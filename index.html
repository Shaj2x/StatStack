Settings & help

Get the app. Add StatStack to your home screen. It opens full-screen and works offline.

How to play

  • Both players are from the same season. Guess whether the right-hand player's stat is higher or lower.
  • A correct guess earns 10 coins × your multiplier. Every 3 in a row raises the multiplier.
  • Spend 2,000 coins on a pack of player cards.
  • Keyboard: ↑ or H for higher, ↓ or L for lower, Enter for the next round. On a phone you can swipe the mystery player up or down.
